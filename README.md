## 📊 Featured Project 1: Customer Churn Analysis

### 🔍 Project Overview
This interactive Power BI dashboard tracks customer attrition metrics and behavioral trends to help the business understand why customers leave and identify high-risk segments to maximize retention.

### 🖥️ Dashboard Preview
![Customer Churn Dashboard](./Customer%20churn%20-1.png)

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **BI Platform:** Power BI
* **Data Sources:** CSV / SQL Database
* **Languages:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) & Power Query (M)

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 7,000 (7K)
* **Churn Rate:** 26.54%
* **Retention Rate:** 73.46%
* **Historical Revenue Lost:** $2.9 Million
* **Lost Monthly Revenue:** $139.13K
* **Average Customer Tenure:** 32 Months

### 💡 Core Insights & Recommendations

* **🚨 High Onboarding Risk:** Over **47.44%** of customer churn occurs within the first 12 months of tenure. The business needs to improve the onboarding experience and introduce first-year loyalty incentives.
* **📜 Contract Vulnerability:** Month-to-month contract holders represent the highest operational risk, accounting for **42.71%** of total churn compared to longer-term contracts.
* **🌐 Service Red Flags:** Fiber Optic internet users experience an exceptionally high churn rate of **41.89%**, indicating potential connectivity issues or pricing dissatisfaction in that specific product tier.
* **💳 Payment Method Friction:** Customers paying via Electronic Check show the highest attrition rate at **45.29%**. Transitioning users toward automated bank transfers or credit cards could drastically stabilize retention.

📊 Featured Project 2:  Retail Sales & Operations Dashboards


### 🔍 Project Overview

This project features a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard suite connected to an SQL database, tracking end-to-end retail metrics across store operations, regional territories, brand profitability, and customer segmentation. It provides a unified command center to identify supply gaps, high-value consumer demographics, and revenue leakage from product returns.

### 🖥️ Dashboard Previews
![📁 Retail Sales & Operations Suite](./Retail-Sales-&-Operations-Dashb...)

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

• BI Platform: Power BI
• Data Sources: MYSQL (Relational Database Schema)
• Languages: SQL (Data Extraction & Transformation), DAX (Calculated Metrics), Power Query (M),Python(Data cleaning).

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

• Total Revenue: $2,393.54 (~$2.4K)
• Gross Profit: $1,431.59 (~$1.4K)
• Profit Margin %: 59.81%
• Total Transactions: 382
• Unique Customers: 90
• Unique Products Sold: 336
• Total Sales Quantity: 351K
• Avg. Purchase Per Customer: 4.24
• Total Return Quantity: 8K

### 💡 Core Insights & Recommendations

• 🏆 Regional Domination: The North West region acts as the primary revenue engine, securing $1,304.50 in total revenue and a 60.05% profit margin across 635 items sold. Central West remains a severe underperformer with only 24 items sold, indicating a need for target marketing or inventory rebalancing.
• 📍 Local Store Variance: Store 11 (Portland) single-handedly drives $882.90 across 140 transactions, making it the most profitable location. Conversely, Store 14 (San Francisco) sits at the bottom, contributing just $46.90 in total revenue.
• 🏷️ Brand & Product Excellence: Tell Tale leads catalog performance with $116.70 in sales and $65.20 in profit, while Super Extra Chunky Peanut Butter stands out cleanly as the single most profitable individual item.
• 💳 Membership Tier Value: Bronze members generate the highest collective value for the business with $830.60 in profit, whereas Silver members hold the highest personal purchase frequency, averaging 5.3 visits.
• 🚨 Late-Year Return Friction: Operational health is strained by Hermanos Red Pepper, the most returned product overall. Total returns spike drastically at the end of the year, peaking with an alarming 71.82% return rate in December.
• 👥 Key Customer Segments: John is the business's highest-value customer, contributing $70 in profit. Demographically, the Medium Income tier (driving 60.21% of revenue) and Professionals form the financial core of the customer base.

### 📊 Featured Project 4: Pizza Business Analytics


### 🔍 Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard tracks sales performance, order volumes, and customer preferences for a pizza restaurant business to help optimize inventory, staffing, and menu offerings.

### 🖥️ Dashboard Preview
![📁 Pizza Analytics Page ](./Pizza%20analytics%20Page/)

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

• BI Platform: Power BI
• Data Sources: CSV / SQL Database
• Languages: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) & Power Query (M)

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

• Total Revenue: $1.54K
• Total Orders: 21K
• Total Sales: $818K
• Total Quantity Ordered: 50K
• Best Selling Pizza: The Thai Chicken Pizza

### 💡 Core Insights & Recommendations

• 🏆 Top Revenue Driver: The Thai Chicken Pizza is the clear standout product, generating $43K in total sales, which is nearly double the revenue of the next highest-selling item.
• 🍕 Balanced Categories: Sales are distributed relatively evenly across the four main categories. Classic leads at 26.91% ($220K), closely followed by Supreme at 25.46% ($208K) and Chicken at 23.96% ($196K).
• 📅 Weekend Peak Demand: Order volume peaks heavily on Friday with 3.5K orders, compared to the weekly low on Monday at 2.8K orders. Scheduling more staff on Fridays will optimize fulfillment.
• 📈 Top Volume Types: The Classic Deluxe (classic_dlx) pizza leads total quantity ordered at 2.5K units, closely rivaled by the Barbecue Chicken, Hawaiian, Pepperoni, Thai Chicken, and California Chicken types which all sit at roughly 2.4K units.

### 📊 Featured Project 5: Global sales analytics dashboard 

### 🔍 Project Overview
This interactive Power BI dashboard tracks regional sales performance, inventory turnover, and product line profit margins for an automotive model distributor to help optimize supply chains and marketing focus.

### 🖥️ Dashboard Preview
![📁 Global Sales Page](./Global%20Sales%20Page/)

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
• BI Platform: Power BI
• Data Sources: Relational SQL Database
• Languages: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) & Power Query (M)

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
• Total Profit: $3.83M
• Profit Margin: 39.84%
• Total Sales: $9.6M
• Most Ordered Product: 1992 Ferrari 360 Spider red
• Sales 2003 & 2004: $0.44M (Exceeded $0.43M goal by +3.39%)

### 💡 Core Insights & Recommendations

• 🏆 Top Revenue Driver: Classic Cars is the clear dominant category, commanding 40.13% ($3.9M) of total sales. Vintage Cars sits as the runner-up at 18.72% ($1.8M).
• 📅 Cyclical Sales Trajectory: Sales volumes peaked heavily in 2004 at 48K units ordered, up from 36K in 2003. However, 2005 shows a sharp drop-off to 20K, indicating a critical need to analyze inventory shortages or market shifts in the final year.
• 📦 Product High-Flyers: The 1992 Ferrari 360 Spider red is the highest individual revenue generator, pulling in $0.28M in sales.
• 📉 Underperforming Inventory: The 1939 Chevrolet Sedan sits at the bottom of the sales matrix, generating only $28K. Marketing efforts or bundling strategies should be deployed to clear out slow-moving bottom 5 stock.
